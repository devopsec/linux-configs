# ISO/IEC 27001 evidence map

Baseline checked 2026-10-28: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 with Amendment 1:2024 (climate action changes). The organization defines its ISMS scope, risk method, treatment, and SoA; do not copy a generic checklist as an organizational decision.

| Requirement/topic | Evidence examples | Assessment focus |
|---|---|---|
| Clauses 4–6 — context, scope, risk, treatment | Scope and interface diagrams, risk method/register, treatment plan, objectives, owners | Verify the boundary and interfaces; examine exclusions and risk decisions. |
| Clauses 7–10 — support, operation, evaluation, improvement | Training, controlled documents, procedures, internal audit, management review, corrective-action records | Verify current approval, planned reviews, assigned actions, and effectiveness follow-up. |
| Annex A organizational controls — 5.9 inventory; 5.15–5.18 access/identity; 5.23 cloud; 5.29–5.30 continuity | Asset and identity records, access reviews, provider responsibility, recovery tests, privacy controls | Trace applicable controls to risk treatment, implementation, ownership, and actual evidence. |
| Annex A technological controls — 8.9 configuration; 8.13–8.14 backup/redundancy; 8.15–8.16 logging/monitoring; 8.25–8.31 development/testing | Baselines, patch and vulnerability results, logs/alerts, key controls, secure-development/change records | Sample effective configurations and operation; follow findings through remediation and retest. |

## ISMS artifacts

Maintain the approved scope, risk assessment, risk-treatment plan, SoA with applicability and rationale, control owners, internal audit records, management review, and corrective actions. Separate requirements in clauses 4–10 from Annex A reference controls. Identify provider-inherited controls and the customer evidence that completes shared responsibility.

Official sources: [ISO/IEC 27001:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) and [Amendment 1:2024](https://www.iso.org/standard/88435.html).
