# HIPAA evidence map

Regulatory text checked 2026-10-28 against the current eCFR for 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. Confirm the current rule text, entity role, service eligibility, and legal interpretations with the responsible privacy/security officer or counsel.

| Requirement/topic | Evidence examples | Assessment focus |
|---|---|---|
| 45 CFR 164.308 — administrative safeguards | Risk analysis/management, assigned security responsibility, workforce access/training/termination, incident procedure, contingency and restore tests, periodic evaluation | Trace risks to controls and residual-risk decisions; verify operation, not just policy existence. |
| 164.310 — physical safeguards | Provider assurance and service-specific shared-responsibility records, endpoint/workstation/media procedures | Separate provider facility controls from customer endpoint, media, and access duties. |
| 164.312(a) — access control | Unique identities, role grants, emergency access procedures, access reviews, authentication controls, and any applicable encryption/decryption specification analysis | Test authorization and emergency access records; an addressable implementation specification requires a documented risk-based analysis and decision, not an automatic exemption. |
| 164.312(b) — audit controls | Audit coverage, log access/retention, review records, monitoring and escalation | Verify ePHI system activity is recorded and examined; use a documented retention rationale rather than assuming one universal technical-log period. |
| 164.312(c)–(e) — integrity, authentication, transmission | Change permissions, integrity checks, identity controls, encryption and transmission protections | Examine each relevant path and the associated risk-based safeguards. |
| Privacy and breach obligations | Data-flow and minimum-necessary procedures, rights handling, incident assessment and notification decisions | Route privacy and breach determinations to responsible experts; preserve decision evidence. |

## Business associates and cloud services

Maintain a current register of vendors/subcontractors that create, receive, maintain, or transmit ePHI, including service scope, BAA status, review date, and incident contact. Verify each specific service's eligibility and agreement terms. An account-level agreement or provider attestation alone does not prove workload compliance.

Official references: [eCFR 45 CFR Part 160](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-160) and [eCFR 45 CFR Part 164](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164).
